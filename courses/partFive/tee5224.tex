% COURSE SYNOPSIS
\subsection*{Course Synopsis}
A comprehensive view of high-speed LAN, MAN and ATM technologies and standards.
Evolution towards broadband integrated services digital network.
\vspace{2ex} \hrule\vspace{2ex}

% ----------------------------------------------------- %
% COURSE TOPICS
\subsection*{Topics}
\begin{outline}
    \1 Data Communications: Introduction, Parallel Transmission, Serial Transmission
    \1 Data Communication Standards
    \1 Reference Models and Layered Protocols
    \1 High Speed Local Area Networks
    \1 High Speed Communication Links
    \1 Performance Evaluation of Communication Networks
\end{outline}
