% COURSE SYNOPSIS
\subsection*{Course Synopsis}
An introduction to stabilised power supplies, small-signal models of differential, single stage, multi-stage and integrated circuit amplifiers, oscillators, wave shaping and switching circuits.
Covers high frequency effects and stability and performance measurement.
Devles into electronic test equipment and verification of principles.
\vspace{2ex} \hrule\vspace{2ex}

% ----------------------------------------------------- %
% COURSE TOPICS
\subsection*{Topics}
\begin{outline}
    \1 Field Effect Transistor
    \1 Biasing of BJTs
    \1 Biasing of FETs and MOSFETs
    \1 Small Signal Analysis of BJTs
    \1 Small Signal Analysis of FETs
    \1 High Frequency Response of FETs and BJTs
    \1 Feedback and Oscillators
    \1 Operational Amplifiers
    \1 Power Amplifiers
\end{outline}
