% COURSE SYNOPSIS
\subsection*{Course Synopsis}
The course covers study methods, communication principles, technical definitions, descriptions and instructions.
It delves into tables and graphs, letters, memoranda and curricula vitae and written reports.
It also covers Word processing and computer jargon, interview techniques, running a meeting, reading, understanding and summarising technical articles.
