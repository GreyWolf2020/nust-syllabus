% COURSE SYNOPSISDESCRIPTION
\subsection*{Course Synopsis}
The module explores calculus in one variable i.e.\ Limits and continuity of functions, Differentiation,
Leibniz’s Rule, L’Hopitals Rule, Elementary functions including hyperbolic functions and their
inverses; Integration – techniques including reduction formulae; Applications – arc-length, area,
volumes, moments of inertia, centroids; Plane polar coordinates; Complex Numbers: Basic algebra; De
Moivre’s theorem; Complex exponentials; Linear Algebra: Vector algebra in 2 and 3 dimensions; Scalar
and vector products; Equations of lines and planes.
\vspace{2ex} \hrule\vspace{2ex}

% ----------------------------------------------------- %
% COURSE TOPICS
\subsection*{Topics}
\begin{outline}
    \1 Calculus in One Variable
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Limits and Continuity of functions
        \item Differentiation
        \item Leibniz's rule, L'Hopital's Rule
        \item Elementary functions including hyperbolic function and their inverse
        \item Integration techniques including reduction formulae
        \item Application of integration \newline arc length, volumes, moments of inertia, centroids, plane polar coordinates
    \end{itemize}
    \1 Complex Numbers
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Basic algebra
        \item De Moivre's theorem
        \item Complex exponentials
    \end{itemize}
    \1 Linear Algebra
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Vector algebra in two and three dimensions
        \item Scalar and Vector products
        \item Equations of line and planes
    \end{itemize}
\end{outline}
