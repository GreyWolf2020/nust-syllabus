% COURSE SYNOPSISDESCRIPTION
\subsection*{Course Synopsis}
Course delves into functions of several variable i.e.\ partial derivatives, chain rules and its applications such as maxima and minima problems, Lagrange multipliers.
It also covers linear algebra i.e.\ matrices, basic operations, rank, inverses.
It also delves into systems of linear equations i.e.\ Gauss elimination, Determinants and their properties, Eigenvalues and eigen vectors and Linear independence.
It covers ordinary differential equations, first order differential equations (separable,linear), integrating factors, linear second order equations with constant coefficients, variation of parameters, systems of equations.
Applications of differential equations to mechanics, physics, engineering
