\subsection*{Course Synopsis}
Reading electronic circuit diagrams.
Recognition of standard electrical and electronic symbols.
Electronic circuits board design, fabrication technology and practice.
Design and fabrication of metal cabinets for projects.
Placement of components on the printed circuit board. Soldering practice.
Placement of project in the cabinet.
Finishing and labelling of electronic equipment.
Interpretation of electrical specifications for equipment.
